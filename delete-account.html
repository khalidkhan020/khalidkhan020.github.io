🗑️ Account Deletion

Alaya Attars

Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

What will be deleted

Submitting this request will permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, gender, fragrance preferences, profession)
  • Your order history
  • All wishlists and saved fragrance blends
  • Your ratings and reviews
  • Your Google Sign-In account link to Alaya Attars

This action is irreversible. Orders already placed and fulfilled cannot be retroactively removed from business records.

Fastest way — delete inside the app

If you still have access to the app, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the Alaya Attars app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Account tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the actions list.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

Your account and all data will be removed immediately.
