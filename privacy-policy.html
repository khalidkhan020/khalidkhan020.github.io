✦ Privacy Policy

Alaya Attars

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

Alaya Attars ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Alaya Attars mobile application available under the app IDs com.alaya.master, com.alaya.users, com.alaya.partners, and com.alaya.wholesaler (collectively the "App"). The App is a fragrance ordering and business management platform for our customers, partners, and internal team.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@alayaattars.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our service:

  • Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google's authentication service.
  • Profile information — Information you voluntarily provide: gender, age group, fragrance preferences, and profession.
  • Order data — Details of orders you place including fragrances selected, quantities, packaging options, discount coupons applied, and delivery address.
  • Wishlist & blend data — Named wishlists and saved fragrance blend configurations stored under your account.
  • Partner application data — If you apply to become a partner, we collect your business name, business type, phone number, city, and an optional message.
  • Usage & crash data — Anonymised analytics events and crash reports collected via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Device location — Approximate location may be requested to pre-fill delivery address fields. This is optional and only used with your permission.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your session securely.
  • To process, track, and manage your fragrance orders.
  • To personalise your catalogue experience (fragrance recommendations, family filters).
  • To display and apply eligible discount coupons for your account type.
  • To store your wishlists, saved blends, and order history.
  • To review and process partner or wholesaler applications.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance via crash reports.
  • To send transactional notifications about your orders (status updates).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud database provided by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is governed by Firestore Security Rules that enforce user-scoped read/write — you can only access your own documents.

Authentication is handled exclusively via Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0). We never store passwords.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to fulfil orders. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase (Google LLC) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — Anonymous usage analytics. Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Crash reporting. No personally identifiable information is attached to crash reports.

6. Coupon & Pricing Data

Discount coupons visible to you depend on your account type (User, Partner, or Wholesaler). We do not display internal cost, margin, or admin-only pricing information to non-admin users. Coupon usage is tracked to prevent fraud and enforce per-user limits.

7. Children's Privacy

Alaya Attars is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate profile information via the Account screen in the App.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent for location access via your device settings at any time.
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling Firebase Analytics through your device's advertising ID settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@alayaattars.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
